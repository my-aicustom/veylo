# Known limits at v0.4.0

- This package has not yet been certified by a real two-device, cross-network call test.
- AI latency depends on network conditions and selected OpenRouter providers.
- Listener-side interpretation quality must be tuned on real TEI-style accents, background noise and business terminology.
- Speaker diarization is not yet a hard requirement in the one-device face-to-face flow; overlapping speech remains a difficult case.
- The name VEYLO is a working codename and is not brand-cleared for public launch.
- Self-hosted production LiveKit requires proper TLS, TURN configuration, firewall rules and operational monitoring beyond the local Docker config.
