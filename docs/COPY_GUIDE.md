# Veylo copy guide

## Voice

Direct, international, concise, human. Explain what happens in the conversation rather than advertising "AI" as the product.

## Preferred language

Use:
- Start a conversation
- Join a room
- Spoken language detected
- Interpretation on / off
- AI mediator
- Original / Interpretation
- We need microphone access to hear you

Avoid:
- revolutionary
- magical
- seamless
- AI-powered everything
- unlock your potential
- next-generation experience
- intelligent synergy
- frictionless ecosystem

## Error copy

Say what failed, what it affects, and one action the user can take.

Bad: `Something went wrong.`

Better: `We lost access to your microphone. Reconnect the device, then choose it again.`

## Interpreter copy

Translation output should preserve intent, quantities, names, currencies, dates, product terms, and commitments. AI-mediated clarification must never be presented as if the human speaker said it.
